Honest FAQ

Straight answers

Yes, downloading and playing are free. The game has optional in-app purchases. Stat and class resets, refining without breaking and offline hunting are available to every player.

Gravity is the game's publisher. Steam installs the game; the free Gravity account (created or linked in-game) holds your character and keeps it synced between PC and mobile.

While you're logged out, Offline Hunting earns your character resources. MVPs, party dungeons, guild wars and PvP are played actively when you're online.

You need Windows, at least 12 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended) and 30 GB of free space. Full requirements are on the Steam page.
